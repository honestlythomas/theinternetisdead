Dead David TTS

Chromium/Edge extension · version 1.1.6

Dead David TTS reads selected webpage text aloud. It prefers the Microsoft David voice when that voice is installed, then falls back to the closest available voice supplied by your browser or operating system.

What it does

  • Adds a floating play/stop control when you select text on a webpage.
  • Reads the current selection using the browser's built-in Web Speech API.
  • Prefers Microsoft David but lets you choose any available speech voice.
  • Provides adjustable speech rate and pitch.
  • Can highlight the current word while speech is playing.
  • Can apply optional lime-green and dark-purple selection colors.
  • Lets you drag the reader control and remembers its position.
  • Supports Shift+Space to read, pause, or resume selected text.

Install it

  1. Download the ZIP and extract it.
  2. Open edge://extensions or chrome://extensions.
  3. Enable Developer mode.
  4. Choose Load unpacked.
  5. Select the extracted dead-david-tts folder.
  6. Refresh any webpages that were already open.

The extension cannot run on protected browser pages such as extension stores, settings pages, or some built-in PDF viewers.

Privacy notice

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Dead David TTS does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, user accounts, or code that sends your selected text or settings to a server operated by theinternetisdead.

Page access: The extension's content script is configured for all URLs and all frames so it can detect selected text and display reader controls on the pages where you use it. This is broad access. The extension reads the text you select and interacts with the visible page only to provide its reading, highlighting, and selection-control features.

Speech processing: Selected text is passed to your browser's speechSynthesis implementation. Voice availability and whether speech is processed locally or by a service depend on the browser, operating system, and voice you choose. The extension itself does not transmit the text over the network.

Local storage: Reader enabled/disabled state, voice choice, rate, pitch, highlighting preference, custom selection-color preference, and the floating control's position are saved with chrome.storage.local on your device. The extension does not request synchronized storage.

Permissions: The manifest requests activeTab to contact the active page when you use popup controls and storage to remember local preferences. It does not request browsing history, cookies, downloads, location, camera, microphone, identity, or payment permissions.

Retention and deletion: Local preferences remain until you change them, clear the extension's site data, or remove the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes its locally stored extension data according to your browser's behavior.

Your choice: You can disable the reader from its popup, restrict its site access in your browser's extension settings, or uninstall it at any time. Avoid granting page access on sensitive sites if you are not comfortable with a text-reading extension running there.
