Context

This site contains recursive art and a weighty symbolic language forged through isolation in the midst of the world we live in.

What follows explores the digital decay of identity: the self fragmented into files, records, diagnoses, usernames, evidence and signals—and the role institutions can play in dehumanizing the people they were constructed to help.

You may encounter flashing imagery, unexpected sound, dense symbolism, personal testimony, gallows humour and artifacts that refuse to explain themselves only once.

This website is art first and foremost. It is also a personal symbolic operating system: a place where one life and its creative effort can be archived, processed, translated and made visible.

This is my place on the internet to prove I exist and document my experience as if it mattered.
